How to read this

    This is the RF acquisition schedule. GNU Radio independently schedules DSP buffers under backpressure. Playback speed and processor latency do not move these acquisition events. No scene truth or emitter activity is reconstructed.

    Use context.json for the full receiver configuration and schedule.json for this compact timeline. Configured events describe the nominal waveform timing; they are not a hardware TX-state log.
